The problem beneath the surface

Abandoned, lost or discarded fishing gear — ghost nets — traps marine life, damages reefs and fouls vessel propellers. Side-scan sonar can map the seafloor, but turning raw acoustic pings into trusted operational intelligence remains difficult. JalVedh must identify man-made hazards, reject natural features, and turn verified anomalies into actionable geotagged reports.

Manual Inspection Burden

Analysts scrub hours of waterfall imagery per survey line.

Natural False Positives

Rock outcrops, sand ripples and ridges mimic debris signatures.

Unstructured Detection Output

Findings rarely become searchable, geotagged JSON or CSV records that teams can share and act on.

Sonar-to-Insight 7-Stage Workflow Pipeline

From ocean depths to actionable intelligence, in seven automated stages.

01

1. Data Acquisition

Survey Vessel, AUV, and Towfish SSS logs, GNSS/IMU track, and sensor metadata.

02

2. SONAR-DAPT Preprocessing

Speckle noise reduction, heave/pitch/roll compensation, TVG normalization, and shadow management.

03

3. Multi-Model AI Engine

YOLO (Fast Boxes), Faster R-CNN (Accurate), and U-Net (Pixel-Level Net Segmentation).

04

4. Confidence & Nature Gate

Class-wise confidence scoring (0-100%) and automatic seabed clutter filtering.

05

5. Geospatial Localization

Sonar ping geometry conversion to Lat/Long coordinates and bounding dimensions ($L \times W$).

06

6. Evidence Pack Exporters

JSON/CSV manifests, GeoJSON map overlays, NMEA Waypoints, KML Dive Plans & Evidence ZIP.

07

7. Field Operations & Divers

Guiding Retrieval Boats, Clearance Diver Teams, and Stakeholder Decision Support.

Core platform capabilities

Real-time AI detection, acoustic shadow reliability scoring, natural clutter filtering, and survey-scale spatial intelligence.

Real-time detection

Bounding boxes and mask outlines over the sonar tile, streamed as pings arrive.

Confidence + reliability

Every anomaly carries a 0–100 confidence and a SURE reliability score.

Natural vs artificial

Nature Gate filters rock outcrops, sand ripples and ridges out of the threat list.

Survey-scale intelligence

Searchable, spatially referenced records across entire sonar campaigns.

MARLIN Neural Engine & SONAR-DAPT Architecture

Deep learning models specialized for side-scan sonar segmentation, denoising, and natural clutter filtering.

Multi-Model Pipeline (YOLO + Faster R-CNN + U-Net)

YOLO (Fast Box Detection), Faster R-CNN (High Accuracy), and U-Net (Pixel-Level Ghost Net Masks) trained on 15,000+ side-scan pings.

SONAR-DAPT Denoising

Domain-adaptive preprocessing that suppresses acoustic speckle noise, normalizes Time-Varied Gain (TVG), and aligns 100kHz / 500kHz pings.

Context-Aware Dual Detection

Context-aware visual reasoning distinguishing "Ghost Net over coral" vs "Ghost Net over sand" to prevent false positives in complex benthic habitats.

OCEAN-LEARN Pipeline

Active learning feedback loop that incorporates analyst validations to fine-tune edge AI weights without manual re-annotation.

Why JalVedh & Stakeholder Impact

Conventional sonar suites stop at visualization. JalVedh reasons about what it sees — shadow geometry, ping-level memory, seafloor context — and hands over an actionable decision to MoES/NIOT authorities, marine conservationists, and retrieval teams.
